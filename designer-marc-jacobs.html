Who he is

Marc Jacobs was born in New York City. He worked at the boutique Charivari, where he met the designer Perry Ellis, who became his mentor. In 1988 he became vice president of women’s design at Perry Ellis.

In 1997 he became creative director of Louis Vuitton, a job he kept until 2013.

Why it matters

His Perry Ellis collection for Spring/Summer 1993, shown in 1992, copied what young people in Seattle were wearing: chiffon with Doc Martens, cashmere thermals, big plaid shirts and baggy nightdresses. Christy Turlington opened the show, and Kate Moss and Kristen McMenamy closed it. The soundtrack was Sonic Youth, Nirvana and L7.

Critics and buyers hated it. Perry Ellis fired him a few weeks later and the collection was never produced. Today it is remembered as one of the most important fashion moments of the decade; the critic Cathy Horyn, who wrote that grunge was “anathema to fashion”, said in 2015 she had changed her mind.
