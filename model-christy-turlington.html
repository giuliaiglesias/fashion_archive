Who she is

Christy Turlington was born in Walnut Creek, California. She began modeling at about 14 after a photographer, Dennie Cody, noticed her in Miami. At 17 she appeared in Duran Duran’s “Notorious” video and on the album cover.

In 1989 she was in Calvin Klein’s Eternity campaign, and at 20 she signed a one-million-dollar contract as the face of Calvin Klein. In 1992 she became the face of Maybelline.

Why she matters

She is in two shows on this site: she was one of the four models in the 1991 Versace finale, and she opened the Perry Ellis grunge show in 1992. In 1993 the Metropolitan Museum of Art named her the “Face of the 20th Century”.

She also studied: she went back to school in 1994 and graduated with honors from New York University in 1999. In 2005 she began working with the charity CARE for maternal health, and in 2010 she directed the documentary No Woman, No Cry and launched the organization Every Mother Counts.
