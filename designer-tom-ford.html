Who he is

Tom Ford was born in Austin, Texas. He joined Gucci in 1990 as an in-house designer, and in 1994 he became its creative director, after the company had been bought by an investment firm. He stayed until 2004.

He is credited with reviving Gucci. His look was sleek, sexy and a little dangerous: velvet, satin, leather and clothes cut close to the body.

Why it matters

Gucci’s Fall/Winter 1995 show in Milan is the moment everyone remembers. It had more than 50 looks, including low-waisted velvet trousers and jewel-colored satin shirts, worn by models like Kate Moss, Shalom Harlow and Amber Valletta. Ford even switched off the backlight so the eye went to the clothes and not to the front row. Madonna wore one of the satin shirts to the 1995 MTV Video Music Awards.

After Gucci he launched his own label in 2005 and later directed films, starting with A Single Man in 2009.
