Who she is

Shalom Harlow was born in Oshawa, in Ontario. She did ballet as a child but decided it was not for her, and she took up tap dancing because she liked the noise it made. At 15 she was discovered at a concert by the band The Cure in Toronto.

In 1995 she became the first winner of the Model of the Year award given by Vogue and VH1. In the mid-1990s she co-hosted MTV’s House of Style, with Amber Valletta.

Why she matters

She is in three shows on this site. She walked Gucci’s Fall 1995 show and Prada’s 1996 show, and in September 1998 she was the star of the finale of Alexander McQueen’s “No. 13”, where she turned on a platform while two robots sprayed her white dress.

She also acted in films, including In & Out in 1997 and Vanilla Sky in 2001. In 2007 Forbes ranked her 13th among the world’s top-earning supermodels.
