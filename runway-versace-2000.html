What happened

The dress was made of green silk chiffon with a leafy tropical print and long sleeves, fastened with a brooch at the front below the navel. The fabric was so sheer that it showed most of the chest. It first walked down the Spring/Summer 2000 runway in Milan in autumn 1999, worn by the American supermodel Amber Valletta.

A few weeks later, on 6 December 1999, it appeared at the Met Gala, worn by Ginger Spice and by Donatella herself.

Why it mattered

In 2000, Jennifer Lopez wore the dress to the 42nd Grammy Awards. It was an instant hit and is now considered a classic of fashion history. It proved that Donatella, who had become the head designer after her brother Gianni was killed in 1997, could create a moment as big as his.
