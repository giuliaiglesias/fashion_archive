Who he is

Valentino Clemente Ludovico Garavani was born in Voghera, Italy. He went back to Rome in 1959 and showed his first collection in his own salon on Via Condotti. The next year he met Giancarlo Giammetti, who became his business partner.

He became known for elegant, very feminine evening wear and for one color above all: a deep red that fashion people simply call “Valentino red”. His clients included Elizabeth Taylor, Audrey Hepburn and Jacqueline Kennedy, who wore one of his ivory designs when she married Aristotle Onassis in 1968.

Why it matters

In the 1990s he was already a legend, one of the last of the great couturiers still designing. In 1998 he and Giammetti sold the company, but Valentino stayed on as its designer until he retired in 2008, the same year a documentary about him, Valentino: The Last Emperor, came out.

He is the starting point of this museum because he represents classic glamour: the idea that a dress can be an event. He died in January 2026.

Moments to know

  1. 1959Shows his first collection in his own salon on Via Condotti, Rome.
  2. 1960Meets Giancarlo Giammetti, who becomes his business partner.
  3. 1968Jacqueline Kennedy wears one of his ivory designs for her wedding to Aristotle Onassis.
  4. 1991Sophia Loren receives her honorary Oscar in a black sequinned Valentino dress.
  5. 1992His couture collection includes the black-and-white dress Julia Roberts will wear to win her Oscar in 2001.
  6. 1998He and Giammetti sell the company. Valentino stays on as its designer.
  7. 2001Julia Roberts wins Best Actress in that dress, and Jennifer Lopez marries in a white lace Valentino gown.
  8. 2004Jennifer Aniston wears the red “Fiesta” dress to a London film premiere.
  9. 2008He retires after a 45-year career, the year of the documentary Valentino: The Last Emperor. His last collection ended with every model in a red dress.
