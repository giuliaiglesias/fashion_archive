Who she is

Amber Valletta was born in Tucson, Arizona. Around the age of 15 her mother enrolled her in a modeling school. In February 1993 she appeared on her first American Vogue cover.

In the 1990s she was the face of Giorgio Armani, Chanel, Escada, Louis Vuitton, Prada, Valentino, Gucci and Versace, and she had cosmetics contracts with Calvin Klein and Elizabeth Arden. In the mid-1990s she co-hosted MTV’s House of Style with Shalom Harlow.

Why she matters

She is in three shows on this site. She walked Tom Ford’s Gucci show in Fall 1995 and Prada’s “Banal Eccentricity” show in 1996. At the Versace Spring/Summer 2000 show she wore the green jungle-print dress that Jennifer Lopez later made famous at the Grammys.

In November 1999 she was on the Vogue Millennium cover as one of the “Modern Muses”. In 2025 she was named a Goodwill Ambassador of the UN Environment Programme, to work on sustainability in fashion.
