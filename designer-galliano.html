Who he is

John Galliano was born in Gibraltar. His 1984 graduate collection at St Martin’s School of Art in London was called “Les Incroyables” and was inspired by the French Revolution. He showed in Paris from 1991.

In 1995 he was appointed to Givenchy, replacing the house’s founder, Hubert de Givenchy. In 1996 he moved to Dior as designer in chief.

Why it matters

His first haute couture collection for Dior was Spring/Summer 1997. The next one, Spring/Summer 1998, was inspired by the heiress Marchesa Casati, and the critic Tim Blanks called it a “cornucopia of excess”. Galliano worked with a team: Michael Howells designed the set and Stephen Jones made the hats.

His shows were famous for being dramatic and romantic, and for setting a mood before anyone saw a dress. He left Dior in 2011; this museum covers the years 1990 to 2009, when he was at his most influential.

Moments to know

  1. 1984Graduates with the collection “Les Incroyables”.
  2. 1991Makes his catwalk debut in Paris.
  3. January 1996Presents his first show for Givenchy, the first British designer at a French fashion house since World War II. Dior hires him within a year.
  4. Spring/Summer 1997His first haute couture collection for Dior.
  5. Spring/Summer 1998A couture show inspired by the heiress Marchesa Casati, with her portrait by Giovanni Boldini on the invitations.
  6. Spring/Summer 2000A show inspired by people without homes that he saw on his daily runs in Paris: threadbare clothes, empty bottles and dangling rubbish.
  7. Autumn/Winter 2000A show imagined as a society wedding, opening with a bishop-shaped gown.
  8. Spring/Summer 2003An “Asian” collection with circus performers after a trip to China and Japan. Dior’s couture division reports a 41 percent rise in yearly revenue.
  9. Autumn/Winter 2004The model Erin O’Connor opens as Nefertiti in silver lamé and gold snakeskin. Galliano calls the shape the “sphinx line”.
  10. Autumn/Winter 2006A couture show in a Renaissance maze, with warrior women in gilded chain mail. Galliano takes his bow in a spacesuit.
