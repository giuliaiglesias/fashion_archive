Who he is

John Galliano was born in Gibraltar. His 1984 graduate collection at St Martin’s School of Art in London was called “Les Incroyables” and was inspired by the French Revolution. He showed in Paris from 1991.

In 1995 he was appointed to Givenchy, replacing the house’s founder, Hubert de Givenchy. In 1996 he moved to Dior as designer in chief.

Why it matters

His first haute couture collection for Dior was Spring/Summer 1997. It was inspired by the Marchesa Luisa Casati and was full of bias-cut silks, see-through layers and baroque decoration, so it looked like a ghostly story from another age.

His shows were famous for being dramatic and romantic, and for setting a mood before anyone saw a dress. He left Dior in 2011; this museum covers the years 1990 to 2009, when he was at his most influential.
