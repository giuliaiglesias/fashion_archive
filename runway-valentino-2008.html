What happened

In January 2008 Valentino showed his last haute couture collection in Paris. It had 74 looks: satin bows, lace, pieces covered in crystals, and floral gowns in a Southern-belle style.

For the finale he came out surrounded by models who all wore the same lipstick-red dress. The actresses Lucy Liu and Uma Thurman sat on either side of his business partner Giancarlo Giammetti, and the designers Miuccia Prada and Alber Elbaz were in the audience too. The applause lasted five minutes. Valentino held back tears as the curtain came down, and he said his only goal had always been to make women beautiful.

Why it mattered

Red was his signature color, and ending with a runway full of one red dress was a way of signing his name for the last time. The show closed a career that began in Rome in 1959, and the same year a documentary about him, Valentino: The Last Emperor, was released.

It is also the end of an era: he was one of the last designers who built a house around the old idea of couture.
