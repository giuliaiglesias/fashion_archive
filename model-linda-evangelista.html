Who she is

Linda Evangelista was born in St. Catharines, Ontario. In 1978 she entered the Miss Teen Niagara contest. She did not win, but an agent from Elite noticed her, and in 1981 she signed with Elite in New York. In 1984 the agency sent her to Paris, and in September 1987 she was on the cover of French Vogue.

In 1988 the photographer Peter Lindbergh persuaded her to cut her hair into a boyish crop, which started a trend around the world. She became known as “the chameleon” because she could change her look so completely.

Why she matters

She is one of the models in the 1991 Versace finale on this site. In 1989 she became the face of Versace, and in 1990 the face of Revlon. In January 1990 she appeared on the British Vogue cover with Naomi Campbell, Cindy Crawford, Tatjana Patitz and Christy Turlington.

Britannica says that many people in the industry see the 1991 Versace show as the debut of the “supermodel”. In 1997 she received a Lifetime Achievement Award from Vogue and VH1, and in 2009 she was in the Metropolitan Museum of Art’s exhibition “The Model as Muse”.
