Who he is

Gianni Versace was born in Reggio Calabria in southern Italy and moved to Milan in 1972, where he worked for other Italian designers. By 1978 he was showing women’s collections under his own name, helped by his sister Donatella and his brother Santo.

His style was loud on purpose: strong colors, gold, prints and tight cuts. He is credited with bringing celebrity culture into fashion, with famous guests in the front row and famous faces in his campaigns.

Why it matters

At his Autumn/Winter 1991 show in Milan, the finale began with George Michael’s song “Freedom! ’90”. Linda Evangelista, Cindy Crawford, Naomi Campbell and Christy Turlington walked out in red, yellow and black cocktail dresses and lip-synched to it, just like in the music video. People still call it a landmark of the supermodel era.

Gianni Versace was shot and killed outside his home in Miami Beach on 15 July 1997. His sister Donatella took over the house.
