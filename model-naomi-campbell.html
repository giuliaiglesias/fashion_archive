Who she is

Naomi Campbell was born in London and was spotted by a modeling scout at 15. At 16 she landed her first big job, for British Elle. In December 1987 she appeared on the cover of British Vogue, in August 1988 on French Vogue, and in September 1991 on Time.

Britannica notes that she was the first Black model on the covers of many of the most prominent Western magazines, and that she has appeared on more than 500 covers in all.

Why she matters

She is one of the faces of the supermodel era. She was in George Michael’s video for “Freedom! ’90” in 1990 and one of the four models in the 1991 Versace finale on this site. She also walked in the Perry Ellis grunge show, and in Prada’s fall 1994 show in a nylon dress.

Away from the runway she started a fashion show in South Africa in 1998 to raise money for the Nelson Mandela Children’s Fund, and in 2005 she founded the charity Fashion for Relief. In 1999 she launched a line of women’s perfumes.
