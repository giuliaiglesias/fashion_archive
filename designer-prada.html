Who she is

Miuccia Prada took over her family’s leather goods shop in Milan in 1978. In the 1980s she started using nylon, a cheap industrial fabric, to make backpacks and bags. A triangular logo came in 1985, and her first ready-to-wear collection followed in 1988.

In 1993 she launched the younger label Miu Miu and set up the Fondazione Prada, an arts foundation.

Why it matters

Prada’s idea was that fashion should make you think. One 1996 collection used avocado greens, muddy browns and prints that looked like 1950s tablecloths. Critics called it “ugly chic”, and it influenced a lot of designers afterwards.

By the mid-1990s nylon had become part of everyday luxury. The model Naomi Campbell walked Prada’s fall 1994 show in a nylon dress.
