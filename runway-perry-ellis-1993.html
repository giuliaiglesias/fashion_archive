What happened

Jacobs borrowed the look of the young musicians and fans of the Seattle music scene and made it luxurious: chiffon worn with Doc Martens, cashmere thermal tops, oversized plaid shirts and baggy nightdresses, with stringy, messy hair. The soundtrack was Sonic Youth, Nirvana and L7.

Christy Turlington opened the show, and Kate Moss and Kristen McMenamy closed it. Tyra Banks, Naomi Campbell, Yasmin Le Bon and Carla Bruni also walked.

Why it mattered

The industry hated it. The critic Cathy Horyn wrote that grunge was “anathema to fashion”, and one buyer called it “grunge garbage”. The next season in Milan, the critic Suzy Menkes handed out badges saying “Grunge is Ghastly”. A few weeks after the show Perry Ellis fired Jacobs and shut the line, so the clothes were never made.

Since then the collection has been seen as one of the most important of the decade. Jacobs started his own label, and in 2015 Horyn said she had changed her mind.
