Who she is

Donatella Versace was born in Reggio Calabria. After her brother Gianni was killed on 15 July 1997, she became the house’s chief designer and a vice president of the board, and she received a 20 percent stake in the company.

She kept the Versace spirit of glamour and made it more rock and roll: blonde, bold and very visible.

Why it matters

Her most famous design of the era is the green silk chiffon dress with a leafy tropical print. It first appeared on the Spring/Summer 2000 runway in Milan in autumn 1999, worn by the model Amber Valletta. It was then worn at the Met Gala in December 1999 by Ginger Spice and by Donatella herself.

In 2000, Jennifer Lopez wore it to the 42nd Grammy Awards, and the dress became an instant classic of fashion history. Donatella shows how a house can survive a tragedy and still stay modern.
