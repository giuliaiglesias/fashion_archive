What happened

In 1997 Jean Paul Gaultier launched his first haute couture line, a collection called “Atmosphere of a Couture Salon”, for the Spring/Summer 1997 season. Haute couture means made-to-measure clothes sewn by hand, and until then Gaultier was known mainly for ready-to-wear and for his playful, rule-breaking ideas.

More than twenty years later, in January 2020, he showed a couture collection that he announced would be his last, saying the world is changing and fashion should adapt.

Why it mattered

This was a big year for French couture: Gaultier launched couture, and John Galliano and Alexander McQueen began at Dior and Givenchy. A Paris museum, the Palais Galliera, later held an exhibition about 1997 as a key year in modern fashion. My sources do not describe the individual looks, so this page stays short.
