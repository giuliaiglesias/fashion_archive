What happened

Near the end of the show the crowd went quiet as George Michael’s “Freedom! ’90” began. Four supermodels walked out together in red, yellow and black cocktail dresses and lip-synched to the song, re-creating their appearance in the music video that David Fincher had directed the year before. Some accounts also name Claudia Schiffer.

The press went wild. There were rumors that each model was paid between £25,000 and £50,000.

Why it mattered

The fashion writer Tim Blanks later called it “a fashion moment of biblical proportions”. It is a symbol of the supermodel era, when models became famous by name, and of Versace’s idea that fashion belongs with music and celebrity.
