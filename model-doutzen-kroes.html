Who she is

Doutzen Kroes was born in Friesland, in the north of the Netherlands. She started modeling in 2003 after she sent snapshots of herself to an agency in Amsterdam. She was soon sent to New York, where Victoria’s Secret picked her.

In 2005 readers of Vogue.com voted her Model of the Year. In 2006 she became a brand ambassador for L’Oréal Paris, and in May 2007 she was on a Vogue cover as one of the “World’s Next Top Models”.

Why she matters

She is a good example of the 2000s model: global, with contracts for beauty and underwear brands. Forbes ranked her 14th among the top-earning supermodels in 2007 and 5th in 2008. In August 2008 Victoria’s Secret confirmed her as an Angel, a title she kept until 2014.

She has also supported the youth HIV prevention charity Dance4Life and a campaign to promote the Frisian language.
