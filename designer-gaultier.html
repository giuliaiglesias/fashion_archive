Who he is

Jean Paul Gaultier was born in Arcueil, near Paris. He showed his first women’s collection under his own name in 1976.

He liked to break fashion rules: men in skirts, underwear worn as outerwear, and models of every age and shape. In 1990 he published an autobiography, À nous deux la mode.

Why it matters

In 1990 he designed the famous conical bras for Madonna’s Blond Ambition tour, which made his name known to people outside fashion. In 1993 he launched perfumes sold in corset-shaped bottles, and in 1997 he presented his first haute couture collection.

Gaultier shows that fashion can be funny and serious at the same time.
