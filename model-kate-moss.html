Who she is

Kate Moss grew up in Croydon, south of London. At 14 she was discovered by Sarah Doukas, who owned the agency Storm. In 1990 the photographer Corinne Day shot her for the magazine The Face, in a stark, natural style that was the opposite of the glamorous supermodels. People began calling her the “anti-supermodel”.

In 1992 she signed with Calvin Klein. In the 1990s she became the face of the thin, pale, relaxed look that followed grunge, which the press nicknamed “heroin chic”.

Why she matters

She is in three shows on this site: she closed the Perry Ellis grunge show with Kristen McMenamy, walked Gucci’s Fall 1995 show, and walked Prada’s “Banal Eccentricity” show in 1996.

Britannica says that by the 2000s she had been on more than 300 magazine covers. In 2007 she launched a collection for the shop Topshop, which reportedly sold out in one day, and a perfume line.
