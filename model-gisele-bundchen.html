Who she is

Gisele Bündchen was born in Horizontina, a small town in the south of Brazil. In 1994, aged 14, she was spotted in a shopping mall by an agent from Elite Model Management. The next year she came second in Brazil and fourth in the world in Elite’s Look of the Year contest, and in 1996 she moved to New York.

In October 1997 she appeared on the cover of Brazilian Vogue.

Why she matters

In July 1999 American Vogue put her on the cover under the headline “Return of the Sexy Model”, and she was named Model of the Year by Vogue and VH1.

In 2000 she signed a contract to be a face of Victoria’s Secret, which lasted until 2007. In 2009 she was named a Goodwill Ambassador of the United Nations Environment Programme.
