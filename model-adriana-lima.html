Who she is

Adriana Lima was born in Salvador, in the Brazilian state of Bahia. At 15 she won the “Supermodel of Brazil” contest run by Ford, and in 1996 she came second in Ford’s Supermodel of the World contest. In 1997 she moved to New York, signed with Elite, and made her runway debut in September at Anna Sui’s show.

Her first magazine cover was Marie Claire Brazil in September 1998. In September 2000 she got her first Vogue covers, Italian Vogue Gioiello and then Vogue Brasil.

Why she matters

She walked in her first Victoria’s Secret show in 1999, at 17, and became an Angel in 2000. She stayed with the brand until she announced her retirement on 8 November 2018, after 18 shows. She opened the show in 2003, 2007, 2008 and 2010.

Between 2003 and 2009 she was the spokesmodel of Maybelline. In 2005 she appeared in the Pirelli Calendar, and in 2009 she returned to the high-fashion runway for Givenchy.
