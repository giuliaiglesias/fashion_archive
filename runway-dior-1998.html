What happened

Galliano based this collection on the heiress Marchesa Casati, and her portrait by the painter Giovanni Boldini appeared on the invitations. The set was designed by Michael Howells and the hats were made by Stephen Jones. The fashion writer Tim Blanks describes the casting of supermodels as an expression of “confident theatricality”.

Why it mattered

Tim Blanks called the collection a “cornucopia of excess”. It shows how Galliano treated couture like theatre: a story, a set, a character and a team of specialists around him. For Dior it also meant that the old house became famous again for its shows.
